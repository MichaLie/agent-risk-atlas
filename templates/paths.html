How to read these two views

1 · Into the paper follows one project from the agent's output through automated checks, your own review, lab co-authors, and external peer review into print. Notice that your three defence switches sit inside the lab (Steps 2–4): peer review is not a switch you control, and referees lack your bench notes, intermediate run folders, and raw data.

2 · Into deskilling follows repeated projects when the agent is mostly right. Switch on + The agent checks first: automated checks protect the paper in front of you while leaving your own judgement unused. Only + You think first and + People and rules keep human and trainee expertise alive.
